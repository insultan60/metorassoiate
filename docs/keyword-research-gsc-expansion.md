# Metro Associates (metroassoc.com) — Google Search Console Keyword Research & Expansion Roadmap

**Date**: September 2026  
**Source Data**: Google Search Console (3 Months Performance Data — Web Search, 828 Total Queries)  
**Primary Objective**: Expand keyword visibility, unlock more impressions, and push high-impression striking-distance queries (positions 11–30) into page 1 to convert impressions into qualified B2B client and candidate clicks.

## 1. Executive Summary & GSC Performance Diagnostics

Analysis of the 828 queries currently triggering impressions for `metroassoc.com` reveals clear search engine behavior:

1. **Topical Authority Established, CTR Lagging**:
   Google recognizes Metro Associates as an authority in engineering recruitment. However, outside of the branded head term (`metro associates`: 82 clicks / 487 impressions), the vast majority of high-impression queries have **0 to 1 click**. This indicates the site is ranking on **page 2, 3, or bottom of page 1** (striking-distance positions 10–35).
   
2. **The "Recruiter" vs. "Staffing" / "Headhunter" / "Agency" Vocabulary Gap**:
   While the site has historically anchored its H1s, URLs, and title tags to `"Civil Engineering Recruiter"`, employers and procurement heads frequently search using:
   - *"Staffing / Staffing Agency"* (429 + 126 impressions)
   - *"Headhunters / Executive Search"* (182 + 55 impressions)
   - *"Recruitment Agency / Recruiting Companies"* (176 + 148 + 101 impressions)
   - *"Placement Agency / Job Placement"* (134 + 131 impressions)
   
3. **Surge in California Sub-Market Demand**:
   Over 10 specific California cities/districts (Moreno Valley, Calexico, Lancaster, Glendale, El Centro, Riverside, Corona, Pasadena, Oceanside, Thousand Oaks) generate **600+ impressions** with zero clicks because the site only has pages for `los-angeles-ca`, `san-diego-ca`, and `san-francisco-ca`.

4. **Emerging High-Value Niches**:
   - **Infrastructure Recruiting**: 230+ combined impressions across `infrastructure recruiter`, `infrastructure recruitment agency`, and `infrastructure recruitment`.
   - **Water Treatment & Environmental Engineering**: Direct B2B interest (`water treatment recruiters`, `environmental engineering recruiters in los angeles`).
   - **Outsourced & Contract Engineering**: `hire outsourced civil engineer` (76 impressions).

## 3. Four Strategic Keyword Clusters to Target

### Cluster A: B2B Commercial Synonyms & Head-Terms (Immediate Impact)
*Goal: Turn 2,000+ monthly national impressions into clicks by optimizing for procurement & client terminology.*

* **Target Queries**:
  - `civil engineering staffing` / `civil engineering staffing agencies`
  - `civil engineering headhunters`
  - `civil engineering recruitment agencies` / `civil engineering recruiting companies`
  - `civil engineer placement agency`
  - `hire outsourced civil engineer` / contract staffing
* **Root Cause of Current Miss**:
  The hub page title is currently: `"Civil Engineering Recruiters, Nationwide | Metro Associates"`. The page copy rarely uses the terms *"headhunters"*, *"staffing agency"*, or *"placement agency"* in headings or body text.
* **Optimization Strategy**:
  1. Add dedicated H2/H3 sections addressing these specific service models:
     - *"Full-Service Civil Engineering Staffing & Placement Agency"*
     - *"Executive Headhunters for Senior Civil Engineering Leaders & Project Managers"*
     - *"Direct Hire, Contract Staffing & Outsourced Engineering Teams"*
  2. Integrate structured FAQ entries targeting:
     - *"What is the difference between a civil engineering recruiter and a civil staffing agency?"*
     - *"How does Metro Associates operate as an engineering headhunter for licensed PEs?"*
     - *"Can engineering firms hire outsourced or contract civil engineers through Metro Associates?"*

### Cluster B: Southern California Sub-Market Expansion
*Goal: Capture the 600+ monthly impressions coming from Southern California municipal/infrastructure hubs.*

* **Target Queries**:
  - Riverside & Moreno Valley & Corona (`civil engineering recruiters in riverside / moreno valley / corona`)
  - Glendale & Pasadena (`civil engineering recruiters in glendale / pasadena`)
  - Lancaster & Thousand Oaks (`civil engineering recruiters in lancaster / thousand oaks`)
  - Imperial Valley (`civil engineering recruiters in calexico / el centro`)
  - Oceanside (`civil engineering recruiters in oceanside`)
* **Why Google is Querying These**:
  California Caltrans Districts (District 7 LA/Ventura, District 8 San Bernardino/Riverside, District 11 San Diego/Imperial) are spending billions under SB 1 and federal IIJA funding. Engineering firms in these sub-regions are facing severe PE shortages and searching locally.
* **Actionable Two-Pronged Approach**:
  1. **Enrich `/civil-engineering-recruiter/los-angeles-ca` and `/san-diego-ca`**:
     - Add explicit coverage areas in the schema and visible copy: *"Covering Greater Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley (Pasadena, Glendale), the Antelope Valley (Lancaster), Ventura County (Thousand Oaks), and the Inland Empire (Riverside, Moreno Valley, Corona)."*
     - Add Caltrans District 7 and District 8 specific agency notes.
  2. **Add Key High-Volume Regional Hub Slugs**:
     - `riverside-ca` (serving the Inland Empire: Riverside, San Bernardino, Moreno Valley, Corona).
     - Link them to the regional Census / District cluster.
